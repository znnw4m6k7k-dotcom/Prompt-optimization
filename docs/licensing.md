# License choice

This project uses Apache License 2.0.

Why Apache-2.0 rather than MIT: it is permissive and business-friendly, but adds an explicit patent grant and patent retaliation clause. That matters for infrastructure that may be embedded in commercial desktop clients and internal platforms.

Why not GPL/AGPL: copyleft would restrict closed-source desktop integration and reduce adoption across the agent ecosystem.

Contributions are accepted under Apache-2.0 unless explicitly stated otherwise. No CLA is required; contributors must have the right to submit their work.

Dependencies must be Apache-2.0 compatible. New dependencies require a license note in the PR. Do not submit code, prompts, or data with unclear provenance.
