# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Prompt Refinery to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems, kind about people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence.
- Give actionable review feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs, or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or community spaces.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers through the private contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or GitHub's private reporting mechanism. Maintainers will review promptly and fairly, protect the reporter's privacy, and take proportionate action, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may face temporary or permanent consequences as determined by other maintainers.
